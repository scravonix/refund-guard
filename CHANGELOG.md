# Changelog

All notable changes to Refund Guard are documented here.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-02

Initial stable release.

- Actual Paid resolution from Steam purchase history when an exact match is available.
- Base-game, edition/package, and standalone DLC classification.
- Separate refund-rule and price-drop evaluation.
- Manual and opt-in automatic scanning.
- Native Steam notifications with duplicate suppression.
- Conservative currency, Store availability, and DLC playtime handling.
- Persistent-state schema and compatibility checks.
- Steam Store/library outage resilience and automatic scan backoff.
